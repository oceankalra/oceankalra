Privacy Policy

How information is collected, used, and protected on oceankalra.com.

Last updated: May 2026

This Privacy Policy outlines how information is collected, used, and protected when accessing oceankalra.com or engaging with any services, digital products, or educational materials available through the site.

1. Site Operator

oceankalra.com is operated by Ocean Kalra, providing educational content, advisory services, digital products (via Oceania Designs), and learning frameworks.

2. Information Collected

A. Information Provided Voluntarily

Information may be submitted when users:

  • Make contact
  • Schedule a session through Calendly
  • Purchase digital products through Gumroad or Etsy
  • Subscribe to future email updates

This may include:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Payment confirmation details (processed externally)
  • Information shared during advisory interactions

B. Automatically Collected Information

oceankalra.com does not currently use analytics tools or advertising trackers.

Basic technical information may be transmitted automatically by a browser, such as device type, browser type, and pages accessed. This information is not linked to identifiable individuals and is used only for essential site functionality.

3. Use of Information

Information may be used to:

  • Deliver digital products
  • Confirm and process purchases
  • Respond to inquiries
  • Facilitate advisory sessions
  • Maintain and improve website functionality
  • Provide optional updates or resources (only when a user opts in)

Personal information is not sold or shared with third‑party marketers.

4. Third‑Party Services

Site Operations supported by external platforms include:

  • Digital product delivery
  • Scheduling advisory sessions
  • Payment

5. Cookies & Tracking

oceankalra.com does not currently use advertising cookies, analytics tracking, AdSense, or behavioral advertising.

Minimal, essential cookies (if any) may be used by the hosting provider solely to support basic site functionality.

6. Data Storage & Security

  • Payment information is not stored on oceankalra.com
  • All payments are processed securely through external platforms.
  • Personal data is not sold, rented, or traded

7. Data Minimization

Only information necessary to operate the website and deliver requested services is collected.

8. Data Retention

Personal information is retained only for as long as required to fulfill the purposes described in this policy.

9. Automated Decision‑Making

No automated decision‑making systems are used to make determinations about users.

10. User Rights (PIPEDA)

Individuals located in Canada may request:

  • Access to personal information
  • Correction of inaccurate information
  • Deletion of personal information
  • Withdrawal of consent for email communication

11. Children’s Privacy

The website is not intended for individuals under the age of 16. No intentional collection of information from individuals under this age occurs.

12. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be revised periodically. The “Last updated” date reflects the most recent version.
