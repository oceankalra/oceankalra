Thought for the Week

A brief reflection or idea to pause with while moving through the week.

🌿Deviation from the Mainstream is seen as a medal of honor and, fashionable in all aspects. The same is not the case with AI when it unfollows Expected Norms. Are we missing something?
🌿Originality and Creativity halt automation-driven recession while reenergizing its counter.
🌿AI enhances human potential, but it cannot extinguish it, only we can.
🌿AI is a source, but its fuel is the water and energy we feed it.
🌿AI maps you, and you master its mapping, while ethics draw the line.
🌿At times when a combination isn't a given, choosing between meaning and time becomes a tough call.
🌿Algorithms think; automation acts. One must precede the other.
